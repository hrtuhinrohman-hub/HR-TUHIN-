About

I Don't Just Edit Videos. I Create Experiences.

Over the past several years I've cut, graded and sound-designed hundreds of projects — from fast-paced social reels to full commercial campaigns. My process starts with the story, not the timeline: pacing, rhythm and emotion come first, technique second.

Every cut is built to hold attention on the platform it's made for, whether that's a 9-second hook on Reels or a 90-second brand film for the big screen. Editable copy — swap in the editor's real background, awards or notable clients here.
